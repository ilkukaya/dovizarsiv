EVDS Terms of Use
All users who access the Electronic Data Distribution System (EVDS) application are deemed
to have accepted the following conditions in addition to the CBRT website terms of use (link:
https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/EN/TCMB+EN/Bottom+Menu/Other/Disclaimer)

1. General Provisions
   •   All content provided in the EVDS application is published by the Central Bank of the
       Republic of Turkey (CBRT) only for informational purposes and does not constitute a
       commitment to any person or organization.
   •   The CBRT reserves the right to make changes to the data compiled in the EVDS
       application or to the design of the application, or to remove the published data,
       without prior notice.
2. Use of Information and Citation
   •   Data provided in the EVDS application can be accessed via web services.
   •   Data provided in the EVDS application may be used and published by third parties
       provided that such data are used with reference.
   •   When data provided in the EVDS application are translated into another language, it
       must be stated that the translation is not officially CBRT’s.
   •   Even when the data provided in the EVDS application are used for commercial
       purposes in another product or publication, the users and subscribers of the product
       shall not be charged therefor.
   •   The content of the EVDS application should by no means be taken as investment
       advice.
3. Disclaimer
   •   The CBRT takes the utmost care to ensure that the data contained in the EVDS
       application are up-to-date, accurate, and complete. However, the CBRT makes no
       commitments and accepts no responsibility regarding the data included in the
       application.
   •   The CBRT cannot be held liable for any direct or indirect damages resulting from the
       use of the application or its inaccessibility. Users are advised to seek expert opinion
       regarding the subject before using the application.
   •   Some of the information contained in the application is provided directly by the
       CBRT, while some is obtained from external sources. For the data obtained from
       external sources, these institutions shall be held responsible, and the CBRT assumes
       no liability for such content.
4. Security, Audit and Legal Protection
   •   The EVDS application is secured by the laws of the Republic of Turkey. Any action
       intended to damage the application, or any attempt to interfere with the data, software,
       or communication codes in the system from outside, is against the law.
   •   The EVDS application and related hardware are continuously monitored and audited.
       Users should be aware that their actions on the application may be recorded, their
       information may be accessed, and, if necessary, may be forwarded to the competent
       authorities.
